01 · Problem

A security product customers were learning to ignore

Certificate Transparency Monitoring alerts customers when a certificate is issued for one of their domains. The product had remained in public beta for seven years, and its alerts mixed potentially meaningful certificate events with routine Cloudflare-managed activity.

The product challenge was not simply to send more alerts. It was to improve signal quality, clarify the experience across plan tiers, and establish a feasible path to general availability.

02 · Evidence

Customer feedback established the priority

I synthesized 14 Cloudflare Community threads, 20 support tickets, Salesforce records, and customer success feedback. Alert fatigue emerged as the primary adoption barrier, ahead of dashboard confusion and unclear email content.

I also queried product data to understand the size of the active customer base, subscription behavior, and how plan-tier decisions would affect the launch. Industry research showed that shorter certificate lifetimes would increase renewal frequency, making noisy alerts a growing problem rather than a temporary one.

03 · Product decision

Filter routine events before asking customers to act

I authored the PRD and a two-phase roadmap that prioritized automatic filtering of Cloudflare-managed certificates and a clearer experience across plan tiers. I used Figma Make to make the proposed flow concrete and align with engineering on scope and feasibility.

  • Phase 1 focused on filtering, clearer alerts, and launch readiness.
  • Phase 2 documented deeper notification-system integration and advanced controls for future development.

04 · Delivery

One launch, several interdependent workstreams

I coordinated work across engineering, customer success, product legal, design, developer documentation, and go-to-market partners. The launch combined backend filtering, alert copy, dashboard changes, documentation, and a customer-facing announcement.

My role: maintain the customer problem and product scope while helping each team understand the decisions, dependencies, and launch criteria that affected its work.

05 · Outcome

General availability with measurable noise reduction

During the initial six-day rollout, the filtering system automatically suppressed 199,394 of 332,694 processed certificate events. That 60% filter rate reduced unnecessary certificate-alert volume before it reached customers.
