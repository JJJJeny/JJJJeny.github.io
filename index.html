NUWA Robotics · Service design

Reusable hotel service workflows for robots

Turned recurring partner needs into reusable onboarding and demonstration flows for hotel scenarios.

Case snapshot

Problem: Sales teams needed a repeatable way to demonstrate service robots in realistic hotel journeys.

Approach: Mapped guest journeys, defined service moments, and built modular workflows in NUWA Roflow.

Result: Standardized three recurring use cases for partner onboarding and demos across Taiwan and the U.S.

AAPD Challenge · UX research

Making everyday bus planning easier to return to

Led research and synthesis for a seven-week redesign focused on reducing homepage overload and improving saved-stop behavior.

Case snapshot

Evidence: 49 survey responses plus interviews and scenario-based usability tasks.

Decision: Prioritized a destination-based stop model after finding that non-fixed trips made route-based favorites less useful.

Recognition: 2023 AAPD Merit and Popular Awards.

NTUST · UX evaluation

Reducing distraction in an in-car interface

Studied cognitive, visual, and manual distraction, then tested a constrained Focus Mode for common driving scenarios.

Case snapshot

Research: Interviews with 10 drivers, journey mapping, and task observation.

Constraint: With no driving simulator, the team paired a driving video or focus task with the interactive panel prototype.

Result: Focus Mode achieved an 86.5 SUS score; the standard mode scored 54.5 and remained an improvement area.
